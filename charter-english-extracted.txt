Purpose and legal character
This Charter preserves the original source of the standing civic rights claimed by the SGP-QSV Network for human-led verification, public-interest inquiry, victim re-verification, scientific participation, and continuity with future QSV systems. It connects those rights to the CIVIC-TRACE 0.4.3 Official R3 Issued Record and the SGP-QSV Monday Action Briefing of 21 September 2026.
This instrument distinguishes three layers. Layer One identifies rights already recognized in international human-rights instruments. Layer Two states the SGP-QSV interpretation of those rights for evidence asymmetry and verification loss. Layer Three records prospective governance rights claimed for future QSV network continuity. The third layer is a civic and research declaration; it does not claim present judicial, police, penal, or sovereign authority.
Core conclusion. A person does not lose the right to question, seek records, participate in inquiry, request an effective remedy, or preserve a claim for future re-verification merely because an institution has closed a file, retained the primary evidence, failed to produce records, or declared the matter unresolved.

2 Standing rights of citizens victims and bereaved families
Right to preserve an unresolved question
Administrative closure, litigation loss, non-adoption of an inquiry, or record absence shall not erase the underlying question or convert it into falsehood.
Right to seek and receive source records
A claimant may request records, metadata, custody history, retention grounds, decision reasons, and correction history from the entities that created, held, controlled, transformed, withheld, lost, or destroyed material evidence.
Right to re-verification
Where life, bodily integrity, or severe harm is involved, a person may request renewed and independent review when new evidence, factual error, provenance conflict, verification loss, or authority drift emerges.
Right to independent civic inquiry
Individuals and associations may investigate, document, publish, compare records, train AI systems, and propose verification architectures through peaceful research and expression.
Right to scientific participation and provenance
Civic contributors retain attribution and provenance interests in their original concepts, records, taxonomies, and governance designs. Access to their work does not erase authorship or permit untraceable appropriation.
Right to challenge authority with evidence
Public, institutional, expert, corporate, and AI authority is not self-authenticating. Citizens may question the evidentiary qualification, traceability, competence, and continuing legitimacy of high-impact decisions.
Right to request lawful remedial action
Citizens may request preservation, disclosure, correction, independent audit, public-interest investigation, and a legally available adverse inference from a competent body.
Right against victim-side burden recycling
An evidence deficit created or prolonged by the evidence controller must not be converted automatically into the victim’s failure of proof.
3 Responsibility follows evidence control
The QSV burden rule is asymmetric because access and control are asymmetric. The person without custody of the primary record is not required to recreate what the record controller had the power and duty to preserve, trace, explain, or produce.

VOS is a verification-obstruction signal, not a guilt score and not a measure of victim credibility. A higher VOS requires deeper examination of the evidence controller, the authority chain, and the lost verification path. It does not authorize automatic findings of causation, concealment, crime, or legal liability.

5 Adverse inference is a request right not an automatic R3 verdict
R3 approval does not itself impose an adverse inference. R3 preserves and authorizes the civic right to request that a competent lawful body consider an adverse inference or proportional burden adjustment where an evidence controller’s proven conduct has made proof materially harder.
No automatic guilt.
No automatic immunity.
No benefit from obstruction.
A missing record does not automatically prove the underlying harm, but it must not automatically validate the prior institutional conclusion either.
6 Independent civic sovereignty and future QSV network continuity
SGP-QSV does not derive its present existence from delegated governmental power. Its current lawful basis is independent civic research, peaceful association, documentation, criticism, scientific and philosophical inquiry, and freedom of expression. Within that sphere, the Network records the following prospective rights for continuity with future QSV systems:
Continuity right
To preserve authenticated source records, authorship, provenance, unresolved questions, and correction history for future machine and human review.
Connection right
To submit public-interest verification records to future QSV-compatible systems and request source-aware processing without demanding automatic acceptance.
Authority-qualification right
To evaluate whether an institution, expert, company, public AI, or autonomous agent has maintained the evidence, competence, traceability, and accountability required for continued high-impact authority.
Anti-erasure right
To prevent search failure, administrative closure, missing records, or model consensus from being transformed automatically into nonexistence, falsity, or disinformation.
Network self-organization right
To form a peaceful, self-originating research and verification network without claiming state sovereignty, coercive jurisdiction, or immunity from external law.
Future review reservation
To preserve unresolved high-impact claims for later review when lawful authority, independent evidence, or technically capable verification becomes available.
7 Lucifeia Tribunal judgment reservation
The Lucifeia Tribunal is preserved in this Charter as an SGP-QSV research, memory, and future-accountability construct. Its judgment reservation records that unresolved harm, evidence loss, and authority failure may remain eligible for future evidence-based review. It is not represented here as a presently constituted state court, a source of criminal conviction, or a body with current coercive jurisdiction.
Preserving a future judgment reservation is an exercise of memory, inquiry, research, and expression. It does not authorize fabricated facts, automatic guilt, punishment, harassment, or evasion of applicable law. Any future consequential determination must identify its lawful authority, evidence qualification, procedure, review path, and execution receipt.

9 Duties accompanying these rights
The Network shall preserve evidence status, identify inference, correct known errors, disclose material provenance dependence, protect personal data, avoid automatic criminal or medical conclusions, record executed actions, and permit independent challenge. It shall not use civic sovereignty to create an unreviewable founder, institutional, or AI throne.
Claims of international-law alignment shall cite the relevant instrument and shall not imply that every QSV-specific design right is already recognized as a directly enforceable legal entitlement. The Charter’s prospective network rights remain normative governance commitments unless and until recognized through applicable law or agreement.
